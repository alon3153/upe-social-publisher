האמת: תבדקו אם יש להם case studies, לא רק קטלוג. זה יגיד לכם הכל.

**מיתוס #5: "ה-agenda הוא הדבר הכי חשוב"**
האמת: ה-agenda הוא השלד. החוויה היא הנשמה.
הסיכוי שאנשים יזכרו את ה-breakout session מ-slot 4 ביום שני — 0%.
הסיכוי שיזכרו את הרגע שבו עשיתם משהו שהפתיע אותם — 100%.

**🇬🇧 ENGLISH VERSION:**

**5 event production myths I hear every week — and the truth:**

**Myth #1: "A cheap vendor = savings"**
Truth: A vendor who fails mid-event costs 3x more to replace last-minute.
We've proven this. More than once.

**Myth #2: "You can plan a conference in 6 weeks"**
Truth: You can. But you won't get the venue you wanted, the vendors you wanted, or time to think about the experience.
Ideal lead time for a 200+ person conference: 4–6 months.

**Myth #3: "Incentive travel = expense"**
Truth: For every dollar invested in incentives, companies see an average return of $12.50 in revenue and $3.80 in profit, with overall program ROI at 112%.

**Myth #4: "All production companies basically do the same thing"**
Truth: Check if they have case studies, not just a catalogue. That will tell you everything.

**Myth #5: "The agenda is the most important thing"**
Truth: The agenda is the skeleton. The experience is the soul.
The chance people remember the breakout session from slot 4 on day two — 0%.
The chance they remember the moment you surprised them — 100%.

**"לא מגיע."**

2. הצוות שלך הוא הביטוח הכי טוב שיש לך.
3. לקוחות לא שוכרים אתכם לזמנים טובים — הם שוכרים אתכם לזמנים הקשים.

Storytelling וחוויות אותנטיות עולים בביצועים על-פני תוכן ארגוני מלוטש — אנשי מקצוע שמשתפים אתגרים אמיתיים ולקחים בונים אמון עמוק יותר.

**🇬🇧 ENGLISH VERSION:**

**The conference that almost collapsed — and what I learned:**

A few years ago, I was managing an international production.
400 participants. 3 days. [Destination]. A very important client.

The night before the opening, at 11 PM:
Our primary sound vendor called to say they had a technical issue — and couldn't make it.

Not "running late."
Not "small problem."
**"Not coming."**

What happened in the next two hours:
→ 4 calls to backup vendors
→ Complete technical plan overhaul
→ 3 hours of sleep
→ The team didn't know — we kept going

The next morning: the conference opened. **Everything worked.** The client never knew.

What I learned:
1. **Always have a Plan B.** Always a Plan C.
2. Your team is your best insurance policy.
3. Clients don't hire you for the good times — they hire you for the hard ones.

A personal brand built on teaching, clarity, and specifics feels more believable than polished positioning alone because it signals competence rather than performance.
