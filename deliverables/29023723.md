| **Chapters** | להוסיף פרקים בכל סרטון (קיימים בפורמט למעלה) | Chapter markers give Google's LLMs a structured map of your video content, making it easier to extract and cite specific segments in AI Overviews. |
| **תיאור** | 300+ מילים, תשובה ישירה ב-2 שורות ראשונות | Write 200 to 300 words structured like a blog introduction. Lead with a direct answer to the video's core question in the first sentence. Name the specific topic, product category, or question explicitly. |
| **אורך סרטון** | 8–12 דקות (AEO sweet spot) | Videos under 3 minutes rarely receive AI citations regardless of their popularity on YouTube itself. |
| **שיבוץ באתר** | לשבץ כל סרטון בדף נחיתה ייעודי + VideoObject schema | Effective video SEO requires synchronized metadata, human-verified transcripts, and dedicated watch pages with VideoObject schema to maximize both search rankings and AI citation frequency. |
| **תגיות כותרת** | keyword ראשי ב-40 התווים הראשונים | Place primary keyword within the first 40 characters (before truncation on mobile). |
| **הפצה צולבת** | לפרסם ב-LinkedIn + Email עם link לדף watch | Distribute content across LinkedIn, Instagram, and email campaigns, then link back to the full video watch page — this drives referral traffic, builds backlinks, and gives AI engines more pathways to discover and index your content. |
