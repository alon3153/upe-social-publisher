## What Is an International Product Launch Event?

A global product launch event is a live, produced experience designed to introduce your product to a target audience across one or more markets — simultaneously or in coordinated sequence. Unlike a domestic launch, an international launch must account for:

- **Multiple languages and cultural expectations** — the same reveal moment must resonate in Berlin, Tel Aviv, and Barcelona.
- **Cross-border logistics** — shipping, customs, supplier coordination, and venue management across jurisdictions.
- **Time-zone synchronised streaming** — for hybrid or multi-site formats where not every audience is in the room.
- **Unified brand narrative** — your product story must land with identical impact, regardless of location.

This is where experience counts. Launching a product across borders adds layers of complexity that domestic launches simply do not have. Uproduction exists to absorb every one of those layers — so your team focuses on the product, not the production.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far in advance should we begin planning an international product launch event?**
For multi-country productions, we recommend a minimum of 12–16 weeks. Flagship international launches of 500+ attendees benefit from 20–24 weeks of planning lead time. We have compressed timelines for urgent briefs — contact us to discuss.

**Can Uproduction manage a launch in countries outside Spain?**
Yes. We specialize in tailored incentive trips, corporate travel solutions and full-service event production worldwide. Our production network spans 130+ destinations. Spain is our home market, but we produce global launches wherever your audience and strategy demand.

**Do you handle the content — launch films, animations, demos — or just the live event?**
Both. Pre-event content production (launch films, animated explainers, social teasers) and live event production are delivered by the same team, ensuring narrative consistency from the first teaser to the last highlight reel.

**What if part of our audience is remote?**
We design hybrid launches as first-class experiences for both audiences — not as an afterthought. Our broadcast team produces the live stream as a separate production layer with its own camera direction, graphics, and interactivity.

**Can you provide references for international product launch events you have produced?**
Yes. References and portfolio details are shared under NDA at the proposal stage. Contact us to begin the conversation.
