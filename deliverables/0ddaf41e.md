### H2: Popular Spain Destinations for Corporate Incentive Travel

#### Barcelona
The go-to for good reason. Barcelona showcases striking Spanish architecture with coastal energy and a lively culinary scene. UPE produces programmes here regularly — from 30-person executive offsites to 300-person gala productions. Key assets: world-class hotel infrastructure, proximity to the coast, exceptional F&B scene, and unrivalled cultural programming.

#### Madrid
Madrid adds historic charm through renowned museums and a social dining culture centred on tapas and late evenings. Ideal for programmes that want a more classic, residential feel. Strong for leadership retreats and partner events.

#### Seville & Andalucía
The dramatic south. Flamenco, Moorish architecture, sherry bodegas, and some of the most spectacular gala venues in Europe (think Real Alcázar courtyard). Best suited for exclusive, high-end reward programmes.

#### The Basque Country (Bilbao + San Sebastián)
Gastronomy-led programmes thrive here. San Sebastián has more Michelin stars per capita than almost anywhere on earth. Ideal for pharma, luxury, finance, and food industry groups.

#### Mallorca & Ibiza
Mediterranean island programmes for groups that want luxury resort settings, yacht experiences, and beach production. Mallorca offers breathtaking landscapes and medieval architecture alongside five-star resort infrastructure.

#### Valencia
Underrated for corporate groups. Strong F&B scene (home of paella), excellent convention infrastructure, beach access, and lower demand than Barcelona = better supplier availability and pricing.

### H2: How UPE Works — The Production Process

We follow a structured production workflow for every Spain incentive programme:

1. **Discovery Call** — We understand your group, budget, objectives, and dates. We ask the questions travel agencies don't: What behaviour are you rewarding? What do you want participants to feel at the closing dinner?
2. **Concept & Proposal** — Within 5 business days, we deliver a full creative concept with destination recommendation, programme narrative, venue options, and a phased budget breakdown.
3. **Contracting & Logistics** — We contract all suppliers, manage payment timelines, and produce a full logistics bible (rooming list, transfer schedule, run-of-show, supplier contact sheet).
4. **Pre-Trip Production Meeting** — A Zoom walkthrough with your team covering every programme moment, contingency plans, and communication protocols.
5. **On-Site Execution** — UPE producers are on the ground from arrival to departure. Your single point of contact throughout.
6. **Post-Event Report** — Participant satisfaction data, photo/video assets, budget reconciliation, and recommendations for the next programme.
