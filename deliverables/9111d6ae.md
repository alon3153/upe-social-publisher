## Section 1 — H2: What Is an International Product Launch Event?

An international product launch event is a purpose-built, live (or hybrid) experience designed to simultaneously introduce a new product or service to multiple markets, audiences, or regions. Unlike a domestic launch, it requires:

- **Multi-country logistics** — venues, vendors, and compliance across borders
- **Multi-audience design** — press, channel partners, enterprise buyers, and internal sales teams often attend the same event
- **Brand consistency at scale** — identical messaging, visual identity, and emotional arc regardless of geography
- **Amplification infrastructure** — live streams, translated assets, social cutdowns, and post-event content ready to deploy within hours

A B2B product launch event is one of the most high-stakes moments in any company's commercial calendar. Unlike a consumer product reveal, B2B launches must simultaneously win over procurement committees, technical evaluators, C-suite decision-makers, and channel partners — often in the same room, in the same two hours. Get it right, and you ignite pipeline momentum, media coverage, and partner confidence in a single stroke.

UPE designs every international launch to win on all three dimensions: the room, the stream, and the week after.

## Section 1 — H2: What Is a Global Product Launch Event?

A global product launch event is a coordinated live experience — or series of simultaneous live experiences — designed to introduce a product to multiple international markets within the same compressed window of time. The goal is to:

- **Create a single, amplified market moment** — rather than a slow, diluted country-by-country rollout
- **Synchronize press, analyst, partner, and buyer awareness** across regions simultaneously
- **Generate a unified brand narrative** that travels across time zones and languages without fragmenting
- **Accelerate pipeline** by converting attendee excitement into commercial action before competitors respond

The strategic logic is compelling: get it right, and you ignite pipeline momentum, media coverage, and partner confidence in a single stroke. Get it wrong, and the market moves on before your sales team can book a follow-up call.

A global launch requires a production partner who thinks in systems, not events.

Most event agencies are regional. They are excellent within one country's vendor ecosystem and largely helpless outside it. UPE is built differently.

A single UPE-produced event that travels across 4–8 cities in 4–8 weeks. Consistent experience, locally adapted. Best for: markets requiring relationship-led engagement — DACH, Nordics, Middle East, APAC.
