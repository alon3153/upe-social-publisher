### The New Regulatory Reality in Spain

Sustainability is no longer a differentiator. In Spain, it's becoming a compliance requirement.

In 2025, Spain transformed its corporate sustainability landscape with Royal Decree 214/2025. Carbon reporting is now a legal requirement impacting thousands of organizations — including event organizers managing significant gatherings. Starting with the 2026 reporting cycle, companies must disclose greenhouse gas emissions from direct operations and purchased energy.

Companies must also submit five-year emissions reduction plans starting in 2026, outlining measurable strategies for cutting carbon output.

For companies holding corporate events — conferences, product launches, incentive trips, annual summits — this means your event's carbon footprint is now potentially part of your regulatory disclosure. UPE ensures you're ready.

---

### What "Carbon Neutral" Actually Means for a Corporate Event

A carbon neutral event is one where the total greenhouse gas emissions generated by the event (travel, venue energy, catering, materials, waste) are calculated, reduced where possible, and then offset through certified projects — so that the net carbon impact is zero.

This is not greenwashing. UPE delivers carbon neutrality through a rigorous three-step process:

**Step 1 — Measure**
Before the event: we establish a carbon budget based on expected delegate travel origins, venue specifications, catering menu, and material production. After the event: we collect actual data (flights taken, energy consumed, waste generated) and calculate total CO₂e emissions using recognised methodologies (GHG Protocol or ISO 14064).

**Step 2 — Reduce**
We apply a reduce-first approach: prioritising venues with renewable energy certification, sourcing locally produced catering, eliminating single-use plastics, digitising delegate materials, and optimising transport logistics. Our production team has a sustainability checklist built into every event brief.

**Step 3 — Offset & Certify**
Residual emissions that cannot be eliminated are offset through certified carbon credits (Gold Standard or Verra VCS). We provide your company with a post-event carbon neutrality certificate and a full ESG reporting package — ready for your sustainability report or stakeholder communications.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a "carbon neutral event" the same as a "sustainable event"?**
Not exactly. A sustainable event minimises its environmental impact. A carbon neutral event goes further: it *quantifies* remaining emissions and *offsets* them to achieve net zero. UPE delivers both — sustainable production practices *plus* certified carbon neutrality.

**Will a carbon neutral approach significantly increase our event budget?**
Sustainable production choices (digital materials, local catering, green venues) often *save* money compared to traditional approaches. The carbon calculation and offset cost for a 200-person event is typically €2,000–8,000 — a small addition to most event budgets, and a significant reputational and regulatory benefit.

**Can you certify events that have already happened?**
Retroactive certification is possible but requires good data. Ideally, engage UPE before the event so we can set up data collection from day one.

**Do you handle events outside Spain?**
Yes — UPE operates globally across 130+ destinations. We apply the same green production standards to events in Israel, across Europe, and worldwide.

**What documentation do we receive?**
A full carbon footprint report, offset certificate from a certified registry, event sustainability narrative, and a set of social/PR assets communicating your achievement.

### La Nueva Realidad Regulatoria en España

La sostenibilidad ha dejado de ser un diferenciador. En España, se está convirtiendo en un requisito de cumplimiento normativo.

En 2025, España transformó su panorama de sostenibilidad corporativa con el Real Decreto 214/2025. El reporting de carbono es ahora una obligación legal que afecta a miles de organizaciones, incluidos los organizadores de grandes eventos. A partir del ciclo de reporte de 2026, las empresas deben divulgar las emisiones de gases de efecto invernadero de sus operaciones directas y de la energía adquirida.

Las empresas también deben presentar planes de reducción de emisiones a cinco años a partir de 2026, con estrategias medibles para reducir su huella de carbono.

Para las empresas que celebran eventos corporativos — conferencias, lanzamientos de producto, viajes de incentivos, cumbres anuales — esto significa que la huella de carbono de tu evento puede ahora formar parte de tu divulgación regulatoria. UPE se asegura de que estés preparado.

### Qué Significa Realmente un "Evento Carbon Neutral"

Un evento carbon neutral es aquel en el que el total de emisiones de gases de efecto invernadero generadas (viajes, energía del venue, catering, materiales, residuos) se calcula, se reduce en la medida de lo posible y se compensa mediante proyectos certificados — para que el impacto neto de carbono sea cero.

Esto no es greenwashing. UPE entrega la neutralidad de carbono mediante un proceso riguroso en tres pasos:

**Paso 1 — Medir**
Antes del evento: establecemos un presupuesto de carbono basado en los orígenes de viaje de los delegados previstos, las especificaciones del venue, el menú de catering y la producción de materiales. Después del evento: recopilamos datos reales y calculamos las emisiones totales de CO₂e utilizando metodologías reconocidas (GHG Protocol o ISO 14064).

**Paso 2 — Reducir**
Aplicamos un enfoque de reducción primero: priorizamos venues con certificación de energía renovable, abastecemos catering de producción local, eliminamos los plásticos de un solo uso, digitalizamos los materiales para delegados y optimizamos la logística de transporte.

**Paso 3 — Compensar y Certificar**
Las emisiones residuales que no pueden eliminarse se compensan mediante créditos de carbono certificados (Gold Standard o Verra VCS). Proporcionamos a tu empresa un certificado de neutralidad de carbono post-evento y un paquete completo de reporting ESG — listo para tu informe de sostenibilidad o comunicaciones con stakeholders.
