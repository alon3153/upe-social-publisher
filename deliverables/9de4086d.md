## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is an international product launch event?**
An international product launch event is a professionally produced corporate event designed to unveil a new product or platform to an audience of partners, clients, press, or internal teams drawn from multiple countries. Barcelona combines direct flight connectivity from 100+ cities, world-class venue infrastructure, an established event production ecosystem, and a climate that allows for both indoor and outdoor programming. It consistently ranks among Europe's top three cities for international corporate events and incentive travel.

**How far in advance should you book an international product launch event in Spain?**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a global product launch event?**
A global product launch event is a coordinated corporate event (or series of events) that introduces a new product to audiences across multiple countries simultaneously. It is designed to maintain a unified brand narrative and production quality while adapting to the cultural, linguistic, and regulatory context of each local market.

**How do you run a product launch event across multiple European cities?**
The most effective model uses centralized creative direction — one brief, one visual identity, one narrative — executed locally by experienced in-market production teams. This ensures brand consistency while allowing cultural and linguistic adaptation per city. A single coordinating producer who is accountable across all markets is essential.

**What compliance requirements apply to pharmaceutical launch events in Europe?**
Pharmaceutical product launch events in Europe are subject to EFPIA (European Federation of Pharmaceutical Industries and Associations) codes, as well as national equivalents (Farmaindustria in Spain, LEEM in France, etc.).
