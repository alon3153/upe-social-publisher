### H2: How do I choose an event production company for a corporate conference?

> **Direct answer:** Evaluate any production company on five criteria: proven experience in your event format and scale, in-house technical capabilities (AV, lighting, staging, live stream), dedicated project management, transparent line-item pricing, and verifiable client references. A company with 1,500+ executed events — like UPE — provides measurable proof of delivery.

**Five-question checklist to ask any production company:**

1. **"Show me three events similar to ours"** — not just aesthetically similar, but same audience size and format.
2. **"Who exactly will be my project manager on event day?"** — avoid firms that rely entirely on day-of freelancers.
3. **"Give me a line-item budget breakdown"** — Lump-sum bids hide 15–30% in undisclosed costs.
4. **"What's your contingency protocol if something goes wrong?"** — experienced companies have documented Crisis Plans.
5. **"Can I speak to your last three clients?"** — a phone call reveals more than any portfolio deck.

**Why this matters in 2026:** The Israel MICE market is valued at USD 2.35 billion in 2026, with increasing competition. The quality gap between production companies has never been larger — the checklist above separates proven operators from inexperienced vendors.
