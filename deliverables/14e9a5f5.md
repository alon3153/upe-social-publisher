**[DRAFT FOR APPROVAL]**

**Unpopular opinion: Most incentive travel programs fail before the trip even starts.**

Not because of the destination. Not because of the logistics.

Because the brief is wrong.

I've reviewed hundreds of incentive travel briefs in my career. The majority look like this:
- "We want a memorable experience"
- "Around 150 people"
- "Somewhere in Europe"
- "Budget: TBD"

That's not a brief. That's a wish.

**The briefs that produce extraordinary results look like this:**
✅ "We want our top 80 sales performers to leave this trip feeling that hitting quota is the most important thing they'll do this year — and that the company sees them."
✅ "We have 12 new senior hires who've never met the founding team. By the end of day 2, we want those barriers gone."
✅ "Our ESG commitments are real. The trip must reflect them — with measurable carbon data we can report to the board."

The MICE industry in 2026 is shaped by purpose-driven incentives aligned with CSR goals, ESG compliance with measurable carbon reporting, and hyper-personalized itineraries — signaling a decisive move away from logistics-led planning toward purpose-driven experience design.

The brief shapes everything: destination, format, length, experience design, and ultimately — ROI.

**If you're planning a 2026 or 2027 incentive program:** Start with the outcome. Build backwards from there.

What does "success" look like 6 months after the trip? That's your brief.
