### 📌 Section 1 — H2: What Is an International Product Launch Event?

An **international product launch event** is a corporate production designed to introduce a new product, service, or brand initiative to audiences across multiple countries or regions — simultaneously or in coordinated sequence. Unlike a domestic launch, the international format requires:

- **Multi-language content** and simultaneous interpretation infrastructure
- **Cross-border logistics** (AV equipment shipping, customs, on-site technical crew)
- **Cultural adaptation** of messaging, entertainment, and F&B
- **Hybrid or multi-venue broadcast** capability for remote attendees
- **C-suite and key media management** at global scale

For CMOs and event leads managing audiences in Europe, APAC, or the Americas, the difference between a domestic event agency and a true international production partner is the difference between a product that lands — and one that gets lost in translation.

### 📚 Rationale Notes (for Alon's review)

The pillar page is structured to capture both target keywords (the page explicitly targets `international product launch event` AND `global product launch event` as near-synonyms, avoiding cannibalisation). Cannibalisation is the hidden tax on sites without a cluster plan — when two pages chase the same query, they split authority and confuse engines about which to rank, so both underperform. By combining both terms on one authoritative pillar with clear differentiation in the FAQ, we avoid this risk.

Original statistics and data are the top natural link-generating asset in Spain's market in 2026 — companies that publish market studies, consumer surveys, or sector trend reports are the ones dominating Spanish SERPs. The "UPE Spain 2024 International Event Trends Report" pitch in the Tier 1 backlink plan is designed to create exactly this asset.

Focusing research around topical content pillars is the most effective way to structure an SEO campaign — broader topics that outline areas of expertise, each closely related to how customers might find the product on Google. The 8-post cluster map above covers all adjacent intent variations without diluting the main pillar.
