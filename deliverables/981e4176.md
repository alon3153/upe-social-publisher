## STRATEGIC FRAMING

Case study videos are the highest-trust format in B2B video marketing and one of the highest-converting content assets — because they show a real customer solving a real problem. Product demonstrations and customer case studies specifically convert viewers into qualified leads at the decision stage of the funnel. In 2026, buyers do their own research before talking to sales — they read reviews, compare competitors, and look for evidence that your product works for companies like theirs. A genuine customer story with specific numbers provides that evidence better than any other content format.

**UPE's play:** Two distinct case studies targeting two distinct buyer personas — Hi-Tech CMO/Head of Events & MICE/HR buyer — each ending with a direct CTA to an existing UPE service page.

## 💡 DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFICATION (לאחר פרסום)

1. **LinkedIn posts** — שיתוף כל סרטון עם native video upload (לא רק לינק) + quote מהלקוח
2. **Email nurture** — שליחת הסרטונים לרשימת ליד קיימת עם subject line: "איך [חברה X] הפיקה כנס ל-800 איש — ראו איך"
3. **Sales enablement** — video your sales team sends in follow-up emails and proposals — a case study clip that matches the prospect's situation exactly improves reply rates by 3–5x
4. **YouTube remarketing** — create audiences based on what percentage of the video the user watched (25%, 50%, 75%, or 95%) — those who watched to the end can be shown the next step in the funnel: demo, case study, conversion offer
5. **Pinned comment** — להוסיף comment עם קישור לדף שירות תחת כל סרטון מיד אחרי פרסום
