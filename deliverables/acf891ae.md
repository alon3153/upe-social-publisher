An international product launch event is a high-stakes, multi-stakeholder production designed to introduce a new product to a target audience across borders. When the audience travels internationally to attend, or when the product itself is designed for multiple markets, the production complexity multiplies: multilingual staging, live streaming infrastructure, simultaneous translation, cross-cultural creative direction, and tight logistics across time zones.

This is not a corporate party. It is a strategic business instrument.

#### [SECTION 2 — The UPE Global Launch Model]

**H2:** How UPE Produces Global Product Launch Events — The Three-Layer Model

**Layer 1 — The Flagship Event (HQ Hub)**
One primary event — typically in a premium European destination like Spain — serves as the flagship. This is where your global leadership team, top-tier press, and strategic partners converge. Maximum production value. Maximum narrative control.

Simultaneously or within a 2-week window, regional events in additional markets (e.g., Frankfurt, London, Warsaw, Tel Aviv) mirror the flagship creative. UPE's network of vetted local production partners in 130+ destinations executes the local version to the same technical standard.

**Layer 3 — Hybrid Broadcast Layer**
A broadcast-quality live stream connects all events in real time — allowing the CEO keynote, the product reveal moment, and the Q&A to be shared globally while each regional audience retains a local, in-person experience.

This model has been refined across 1,500+ events since UPE's founding in 2010.
