**🇬🇧 English:**

---

An opinion not everyone wants to hear:

**Most corporate events are a waste of money.**

Not because they're bad. Because they're impressive before — and don't work after.

1. What will change in our company in the week after the event?

מה הטעות שהכי לימדה אתכם בניהול ספקים אירועים? 👇
