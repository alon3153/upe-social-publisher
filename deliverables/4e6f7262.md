## Critical Distribution Rules (2026 Best Practice)

1. **Tag the client company page** in every post — this extends reach to their network and adds social proof.
2. **Post the HE version from the CEO/founder's personal profile**, not just the company page — personal posts travel further on LinkedIn's algorithm.
3. **Pin the best-performing case study** in the LinkedIn Featured section of the company page and the CEO's profile.
4. **Link all 3 landing pages to each other** with internal links (as shown above) — this feeds Google's and AI search engines' topical authority signals.
5. **Send the case study links to sales prospects** at the consideration stage via direct message — not as a cold opener, but as a "thought you'd find this relevant" follow-up.
