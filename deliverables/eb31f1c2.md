## SECTION 2 — What Is an International Product Launch Event?

An **international product launch event** is a coordinated, live corporate experience that introduces a new product, service, or brand initiative to a cross-border audience — media, partners, distributors, or end customers — simultaneously or in staged rollout across multiple markets.

Unlike a domestic launch, an international event must reconcile:

- **Multi-market logistics** — travel, visas, accommodation, and ground transfers across countries
- **Multi-language delivery** — simultaneous interpretation, multilingual AV, localised collateral
- **Cross-timezone live streaming** — ensuring remote markets receive the same emotional impact
- **Brand consistency** — identical staging standards regardless of country or venue

By 2026, hybrid physical and digital experiences have become the standard, blending the intimacy of in-person events with the scale of livestreamed reveals. UPE delivers both: an impeccable on-site experience for your VIP audience, and a broadcast-quality livestream for global markets.
