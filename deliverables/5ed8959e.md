### H2: What Is an International Product Launch Event?

A B2B product launch event is one of the most high-stakes moments in any company's commercial calendar. Unlike a consumer product reveal, B2B launches must simultaneously win over procurement committees, technical evaluators, C-suite decision-makers, and channel partners — often in the same room, in the same two hours.

Get it right, and you ignite pipeline momentum, media coverage, and partner confidence in a single stroke. Get it wrong, and the market moves on before your sales team can book a follow-up call.

At UPE, we engineer product launch events that are **commercially precise and experientially unforgettable** — from venue scouting and creative concept through to live production, hybrid streaming, and post-event analytics.

---

### H2: Launch Formats We Produce

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all. Every product launch we build starts with a question: *What does this audience need to feel, believe, and do by the time they walk out?*

---

#### H3: 🎤 Conference-Style Launch Event
**Best for:** SaaS platforms, tech products, enterprise software, telecom solutions

This "big moment" format — a half-day or full-day conference where the product reveal is the centrepiece — combines keynote presentations, live product demonstrations, customer success panels, industry fireside chats, and structured networking. The conference format works especially well when you want to establish category leadership alongside the launch, not just announce a feature set.

UPE handles full production: AV, stage design, content direction, speaker coaching, logistics, and event-day management.

---

#### H3: 🌐 Hybrid Global Launch
**Best for:** Global or national B2B audiences, remote-first organisations, and products targeting geographically dispersed buyer committees.

A hybrid launch combines a live, high-production in-person event with a simultaneously streamed virtual experience. Remote attendees participate in live polls, Q&A, and breakout sessions while the in-room audience gets hands-on demos.

UPE integrates real-time streaming, multilingual moderation, and cross-site coordination from a single production team. One brief. One vision. Every market covered.

---

#### H3: 🥂 Executive Unveil & Private Summit
**Best for:** High-ticket B2B products, exclusive partner reveals, investor announcements

An intimate, curated experience — typically 50–200 attendees — focused on conversion and relationship depth. These events emphasise informal interaction through structured networking activities, cocktail receptions, and discussion panels, combined with precision-designed product demonstration moments.

---

#### H3: 🏖 Incentive-Integrated Launch Trip
**Best for:** Channel partner launches, sales force activation, international roadshows

Merge your product launch with an incentive travel experience. A counterweight to digital saturation, wellness-first programme design is reshaping incentive trips and senior-level retreats, as over-packed agendas give way to fewer sessions, more reflection, and intentional value-building windows.

UPE's incentive travel expertise across 130+ destinations means your product reveal becomes a memory your partners carry for years.

**Q: How far in advance should we start planning an international product launch event?**

A: For events with 200+ attendees in international destinations, we recommend engaging UPE at least **16–20 weeks** before the target date. Venue exclusivity, visa logistics for multi-country delegations, and production lead times make early engagement essential. For events under 100 pax, 10–12 weeks is workable.

---

**Q: Can UPE manage a product launch event across multiple European cities simultaneously?**

A: Yes — multi-city simultaneous launches are a UPE speciality. We coordinate unified creative direction, separate on-ground production teams, and live cross-site streaming so that your brand message lands with identical force in Frankfurt, London, and Tel Aviv at the same moment.

---

**Q: Do you work with Israeli companies launching products in Europe?**

A: Absolutely. The Israel–Europe axis is one of our core operational strengths. We understand the commercial culture, regulatory requirements, and logistical specifics of both markets. Many of our clients are Israeli tech and pharma companies executing European rollouts.

**Q: What size product launch events does UPE typically produce?**

A: We have produced launch events for as few as 30 C-suite executives and as many as 3,000+ multi-market participants. Our sweet spot for international product launches is typically **150–800 attendees** with hybrid reach of 1,000–5,000 remote viewers.

**Q: Can a product launch be integrated with an incentive trip?**

A: Yes, and we strongly encourage it when your target audience is a partner or reseller network. Combining the launch reveal with an incentive travel experience dramatically increases engagement, recall, and commercial follow-through. **Q: What European destinations do you recommend for international product launches?**
