**Most event briefs I receive are missing the one thing that makes events actually work.**

After 16 years and 1,500+ events at UPE — across Israel, Europe, and 130+ global destinations — the brief that produces the best outcomes looks nothing like what most companies send.

Most briefs I receive include:
- Dates ✓
- Headcount ✓
- Budget range ✓
- "We want something different" ✓ (everyone writes this)

What the best briefs include:

**→ The "day after" picture**
What specific behavior, decision, or mindset shift do you want to see 30 days after the event?

**→ The tension**
What's the organizational challenge this event needs to help resolve? Misaligned teams? Post-merger culture clash? Low morale after a hard year?

**→ The "non-negotiable feeling"**
Not "inspiring" or "memorable." Something specific: *"People should leave feeling like they're part of something that's actually going to win."*

**→ The skeptic**
Who in the room will be hardest to move — and what would move them?

Give us these four things, and we can produce something that actually matters.

HR and Finance are increasingly in the same room when this budget is approved.
Companies that can articulate incentive ROI in terms of retention, productivity, or NPS will keep their programs.
Companies that can't will see budgets cut first.

**4. From "standard group" to "hyper-personalization"**
130+ destinations and 25,000+ participants later — the details that create loyalty are almost always personal.
*The note in the room. The activity that wasn't on the itinerary.*
