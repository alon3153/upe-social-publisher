- **Brand storytelling** backdrop — Architecture, light, and culture that make every product reveal cinematic
- **Cost efficiency** — Premium production at 20–30% lower cost than equivalent events in Paris or Zurich

Whether you're launching a B2B SaaS platform, a consumer tech device, a pharmaceutical product, or a global brand campaign, Spain gives you the canvas. UPE gives you the production.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**How far in advance should I book a product launch event in Spain?**
For events of 200+ attendees, we recommend a 12–16 week lead time. Smaller events (under 100 guests) can often be produced in 8 weeks. Peak season in Barcelona and Madrid (April–June, September–November) books up fast — the earlier you engage us, the better your venue options.

**Can UPE produce a hybrid product launch — live in Spain and streamed globally?**
Yes. Hybrid production is a core capability. We handle the in-room experience and the full streaming setup, including multilingual streams, online audience engagement tools, and post-event recording delivery.

**Do you work with companies headquartered outside Spain?**
Absolutely. A significant share of our clients are Israeli, European, and North American companies using Spain as a launch destination. We provide full end-to-end coordination so your internal team doesn't need to manage local suppliers directly.

**What does a product launch event in Spain cost?**
Production costs vary significantly by scale, format, and specification. A high-quality press preview for 50–80 guests typically starts from €25,000. A flagship launch event for 500+ attendees ranges from €80,000 to €250,000+. We provide a detailed, itemized proposal after our discovery call.

### H1: New Hire Team Building in Spain — Turn Day One Into a Moment They Never Forget

---

### [INTRO — ~100 words]

The first 90 days define whether a new hire stays or leaves. Research consistently shows that employees who feel genuinely connected to their team and company culture are dramatically more likely to become long-term contributors. A **new hire team building event** isn't a nice-to-have — it's one of the highest-ROI investments an HR or People team can make.

UPE designs and produces new hire team building experiences in Spain that go far beyond a city tour and dinner. Delivered across Barcelona, Madrid, Seville, and beyond.

---

### H2: Why New Hire Team Building Matters More Than Ever

Remote-first and hybrid hiring means new employees often join companies without ever meeting their colleagues in person. When they do finally gather — for an onboarding sprint, a quarterly kickoff, or a cohort integration week — the experience has to do serious work.

Effective new hire team building:
- **Reduces early attrition** — Employees who feel connected in month one are significantly less likely to leave in year one
- **Accelerates time-to-productivity** — Psychological safety enables faster knowledge sharing, question-asking, and collaboration
- **Embeds company culture** — Values are lived, not read, when they're woven into shared experiences
- **Breaks silos before they form** — Cross-functional cohorts build inter-departmental relationships from day one
- **Strengthens employer brand** — New hires who have a great onboarding experience become internal and external advocates

Spain is one of Europe's most powerful settings for this kind of experience: the warmth, the food, the architecture, the outdoor possibilities — all of it works in service of human connection in a way that a conference room in an office park simply cannot replicate.

### H2: New Hire Team Building Programs UPE Produces

We don't offer a menu of generic activities. We design a program architecture that fits your company size, culture, and goals — then produce it end-to-end.

#### H3: City Discovery Challenges (Barcelona / Madrid / Seville)
Small cross-functional teams navigate the city together — solving location-based challenges, completing creative missions, and collaborating under time pressure. GPS-enabled, fully facilitated, and designed to surface team dynamics in a fun, pressure-free environment. Scales from 20 to 500+ participants.

#### H3: Collaborative Creation Workshops
Teams build something together — a product prototype, a brand campaign, a film, a dish, a piece of music. The output doesn't matter; the process does. Facilitated by our certified trainers to extract real insight about communication styles, leadership, and group dynamics.

#### H3: Values Activation Sessions
We take your company's stated values and translate them into structured experiences. Each team completes challenges designed to require the behaviors your culture demands — whether that's innovation, customer empathy, inclusion, or accountability. Debrief sessions anchor the learning.

#### H3: Outdoor & Adventure Integration
Spain's natural landscape — from the Pyrenees to the Costa Brava — offers extraordinary settings for adventure-based team building. Kayaking challenges, orienteering, coastal hiking relays, and sailing regattas create shared struggle and shared triumph in ways that office environments can't replicate.

#### H3: Gastronomy & Culture Experiences
A paella-making competition. A wine and olive oil tasting challenge. A flamenco workshop turned performance. These experiences are built around joy — and joy creates memory. When new hires remember the company that gave them that afternoon in a Barcelona market, they carry the employer brand differently.

#### H3: Multi-Day Onboarding Retreats
For companies bringing in large cohorts or integrating teams from multiple countries, we produce full 2–3 day onboarding retreats: hotel or resort block, structured day programs alternating between team building and business content, evening experiences, and full logistics. We manage everything — you show up and lead.

### H2: How UPE Designs Your New Hire Program

We start with your people strategy, not our activity catalogue.

**Discovery** — What are the backgrounds of your new hires? What teams will they be joining? What does your leadership want them to feel, know, and believe after this experience? What has worked before, and what hasn't?

**Program Architecture** — We design an experience flow: the right mix of structured and unstructured time, high-energy and reflective moments, competitive and collaborative tasks. We map this to your timeline and budget.

**Location & Venue** — We scout and negotiate the venue that fits the program: a beachfront hotel for a sailing-to-gastronomía flow; an urban loft for a city-challenge-to-workshop day; a countryside finca for a retreat format.

**Facilitation** — Our certified facilitators run the experience on the day. They manage energy, handle the unexpected, and extract meaning from every moment.

**Content & Logistics** — Branded materials, participant kits, catering, transportation, accommodation coordination. Everything aligned to your brand.

**Post-Event Report** — Participant feedback, photos, video highlights, and recommendations for the next cohort.

### H2: Why Spain — and Why Barcelona or Madrid Specifically?

Spain is more than a beautiful backdrop. It's a strategic advantage for new hire team building because:

- **Accessible** — Direct flights from across Europe, Israel, and North America make it easy to gather dispersed teams
- **Energizing** — The climate, the culture, and the cuisine create a sensory environment that primes people to open up and connect
- **Diverse venue options** — Urban venues, beach resorts, mountain retreats, and historic estates within 1–2 hours of each other
- **Operationally mature** — Hospitality infrastructure that can handle complex group logistics reliably

Barcelona in particular offers an unmatched density of options: the Gothic Quarter for cultural discovery, Montjuïc for outdoor challenges, Barceloneta for water-based activities, and Poblenou for modern creative studios.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**How many people can attend a new hire team building event?**
UPE produces programs for groups from 20 to 500+ participants. For very large cohorts, we design parallel-track programs that run simultaneously, then converge for shared experiences.

**Can the program be delivered in multiple languages?**
Yes. We regularly produce bilingual and trilingual programs — English, Spanish, Hebrew, French, and German are our most common working languages. Facilitation, printed materials, and digital content can all be localized.

**How far in advance do I need to book?**
For groups under 50, 6–8 weeks is usually sufficient. For larger retreats or multi-day programs, 10–14 weeks is ideal. We can sometimes accommodate urgent requests — contact us to discuss your timeline.

**Can you combine new hire team building with a company all-hands or kickoff event?**
Absolutely. Many of our clients embed a new hire team building module within a broader company event — the cohort-specific integration happens in the morning, and the all-hands kicks off in the afternoon. We design the full arc.

**What makes UPE different from a local activities provider?**
We are a full production company, not an activities vendor. We manage strategy, design, logistics, facilitation, and measurement. You get one point of contact, not a patchwork of suppliers.
